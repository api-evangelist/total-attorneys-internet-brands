# Total Attorneys (Internet Brands)

> Total Attorneys is a legal marketing and lead-generation brand owned by Internet Brands. Originally a legal practice-management and client-intake SaaS, the Total Attorneys service was folded into the Martindale-Avvo network, and as of April 1, 2019 its attorney lead-generation offering transitioned to the Martindale-Nolo Pay Per Lead program. The totalattorneys.com site today markets pay-per-lead attorney marketing across practice areas such as bankruptcy, criminal defense, DUI/DWI, divorce, and personal injury. No public developer program or API is currently published.
